# Events

## Browsing Events

Visit the events page to see all upcoming events. Use the search bar, event type dropdown, and subspecialty filter to find what you need. Toggle between **list** and **calendar** views.

## Event Details

Click an event to see full details: description, timetable (single or multi-day), faculty and speakers, location with map link, and pricing (standard vs. member rate).

## Registering for Events

#### Online Events

**Webinars and Online Lectures:**

1. Click **Register for Webinar**.
2. Once approved, a join button and meeting details (Zoom link, Meeting ID, Passcode) appear on the event page.

#### Application-Based Events

**Workshops and In Person Courses:**

1. Fill in the application form (motivation statement, answers to custom questions).
2. Submit your application before the deadline.
3. Check your status: Pending → Approved / Rejected / Waitlisted.
4. You will receive an email when your status changes.
5. If approved, confirm your attendance.

#### Full Member Events

**In-person courses for Full Members:**

Some in-person courses require a verified ACPGBI membership number. Add your number in your [Profile](member-portal.md#acpgbi-membership) to gain access.

#### The Dukes Weekend

The Dukes Weekend runs Friday to Sunday and is open to Dukes' Club members only. You can book Friday, Saturday, or both, and add Sunday alongside Saturday — Sunday cannot be booked on its own.

1. **Choose your days.** Friday and Sunday have courses; Saturday is the main programme. If the weekend is being streamed, you can choose to join Saturday online instead of in person.
2. **Confirm your booking**, then come back and **choose your courses**. You can take one course per time slot — anything that clashes with a course you already hold is shown greyed out with the reason. If a course is full you can join its waitlist, and we will email you if a place opens up.
3. **Say whether you need a room** for Friday and/or Saturday night. Rooms are allocated by the Dukes' Club, so you are telling us you need a bed rather than picking one. A Friday night room is only available if you are booked onto a Friday course, and a Saturday night room needs you there in person.

A single **refundable deposit** covers the whole weekend, however many days or courses you book — booking more does not cost more. It is returned after the event.

You can change your days, swap courses, and release places at any point until bookings close. Dropping a day releases that day's course places, and if you drop your last Friday course your Friday night room request goes with it — we will email you if that happens.

After the weekend, each course you attended has its own short feedback form, and there is one for the weekend overall.

## Cancelling

You can cancel a registration from the event page at any time.
