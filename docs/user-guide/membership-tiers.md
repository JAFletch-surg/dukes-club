# Membership Tiers

| Tier              | How to get it                                     | What you can access                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Guest**         | No account needed                                 | Public pages: Home, About, Contact, Events listing, News                                                     |
| **Trainee**       | Register and get approved                         | Member portal, webinars, podcasts, directory, messaging, 7-day question bank trial                           |
| **Full Member**   | Add a verified ACPGBI number on your profile      | Everything above, plus: unlimited question bank, in-person course booking, certificates & CPD tracking       |

> **Note:** Tiers are separate from the category you register under. **International** members —
> those based outside the UK and Ireland — sit on the same tiers as UK trainees, but every
> international registration is reviewed by an administrator before it is approved. Your country
> appears in the [Member Directory](directory-and-messages.md) where a UK member's deanery would.

> **Tip:** Go to your [Profile](member-portal.md#profile) and enter your ACPGBI membership number under the ACPGBI Membership section. Once verified, you unlock all Full Member features.
