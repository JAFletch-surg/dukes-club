# Membership Tiers

| Tier              | How to get it                                     | What you can access                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Guest**         | No account needed                                 | Public pages: Home, About, Contact, Events listing, News                                                     |
| **Trainee**       | Register and get approved                         | Member portal, webinars, podcasts, directory, messaging, 7-day question bank trial, 1-month preview of the members-only videos |
| **Full Member**   | Add a verified ACPGBI number on your profile      | Everything above, plus: unlimited question bank, the full video archive, in-person course booking, certificates & CPD tracking |

> **Note:** Tiers are separate from the category you register under. **International** members —
> those based outside the UK and Ireland — sit on the same tiers as UK trainees, but every
> international registration is reviewed by an administrator before it is approved. Your country
> appears in the [Member Directory](directory-and-messages.md) where a UK member's deanery would.
> International members keep access to the [members-only videos](videos-and-podcasts.md#who-can-watch-what)
> rather than losing them at the end of the trainee preview.

> **Tip:** Go to your [Profile](member-portal.md#profile) and enter your ACPGBI membership number under the ACPGBI Membership section. Once verified, you unlock all Full Member features. No number yet? The same section — and every locked feature that asks for one — has a **Get an ACPGBI Membership Number** button that takes you to ACPGBI to apply.
