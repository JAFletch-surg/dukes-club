# Email Preferences

## The Round-Up

The round-up is a regular email highlighting upcoming events, new posts and new videos from the
site, with links straight to each one. You are subscribed weekly by default, and you can change or stop it at any
time.

## Changing your settings

Go to **My Profile → Email Updates** in the members' area, or use the **Choose what you hear about**
link at the bottom of any round-up email — that one works without signing in.

You can set:

* **How often** — Weekly, Fortnightly (every 2 weeks), Monthly (every 4 weeks), or Never.
* **What it includes** — Upcoming events, news and blog posts, and new videos from the members'
  video library. Switch any of them off individually.
* **Which news categories** — Leave all unselected to receive everything, or pick specific
  categories such as Careers or Research to narrow it down.

Click **Save Preferences** when you are done.

## Unsubscribing

Choose **Never** in your preferences, or use the **Unsubscribe** link in the email footer. Your mail
app may also show its own unsubscribe button next to the sender's name, which works the same way.

Unsubscribing takes effect immediately. If you would rather just hear from us less often, changing
the frequency to monthly is usually a better option than opting out altogether — you can switch back
whenever you like from the same link.

## Emails you will still receive

Preferences only control the round-up. Emails about your own account are always sent:

* Event booking confirmations and status updates
* Certificates and feedback requests
* Password resets and account approval notices

## What you will not receive

You will never be sent an empty round-up. If there are no upcoming events and no posts or videos
published since you last heard from us, that edition is skipped — and the items still appear in your
next one.

Videos work like news: you are shown a video once, in the first round-up after it is published, so
the same lecture is never sent to you twice.
