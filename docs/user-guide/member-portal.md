# Member Portal

After logging in, you can access the member portal at `/members`.

## Dashboard

An overview of your activity: upcoming events, recent videos, exam prep stats, and quick links to key sections.

## Giving feedback on the site

From time to time a card appears on your dashboard inviting you to fill in a short questionnaire
about the members' area. It takes about three minutes and asks how the site looks and works, how
useful you find each part of it, and what you'd like us to build next. Your answers go to the
Dukes' Club committee and genuinely shape what gets built.

Your progress is saved as you go, so you can close the tab and come back to it. Once you've
answered, the card disappears — we'll invite you again next time we're planning.

There's a **"don't show my name against my answers"** option at the end. Turn it on and the
committee sees your answers without your name, email or role attached. We still record that
you've responded, so you aren't asked twice, and your answers are still tagged with your training
grade and region so we can see how needs differ across the membership — on a club this size that
may still narrow things down, so it's worth knowing before you write something you'd rather
nobody traced back to you.

## Profile

Manage your account from the profile page.

### Personal Info

Update your name, hospital, region, training stage, subspecialty interests, and social links (Twitter/X, LinkedIn).

### ACPGBI Membership

* Enter your ACPGBI membership number to unlock [Full Member](membership-tiers.md) access.
* Status shows as **Full Member** (verified), **Pending Verification**, or a prompt to submit your number.

> **Warning:** Without a verified ACPGBI number, access to the question bank expires after a 7-day trial and in-person course booking is unavailable.

### Privacy Settings

* Toggle directory visibility on or off.
* When visible, control what others can see: email, hospital, region, training stage, subspecialties, social links, and whether you accept direct messages.

### Account

* Change your password.
* Delete your account (with confirmation).
