# Getting Started

## Creating an Account

1. Click **Register** and choose the category you are registering under:
   * **UK / Ireland** — you are training or working in a UK or Irish deanery.
   * **International** — you are based anywhere else.
2. Fill in:
   * Full name, email, and password.
   * **Training stage** (FY1–ST8, Post-CCT, Consultant, SAS, Academic, or Other).
   * **Deanery / Region** (16 UK and Ireland regions) if you registered as UK / Ireland, or
     **Country** if you registered as International.
   * **GMC number** — asked for only if you are a UK / Ireland registrant using an email we can't
     recognise (see Auto-Approval below). It is your 7-digit GMC reference number, and an admin uses
     it to confirm your identity before approving the account.
   * **ACPGBI membership number** (optional — enter this to unlock Full Member features).
   * **Directory visibility** — choose whether to appear in the Member Directory.
3. Submit the form and check your email for a verification link.

## Auto-Approval

> **Note:** If you register as **UK / Ireland** with an **NHS** (.nhs.net, .nhs.uk),
> **doctors.org.uk**, or **.ac.uk** email address, your account is approved automatically as a
> Trainee. Other email domains require manual admin approval, which typically takes 1–2 working days.

> **International members:** international registrations are always reviewed by an administrator,
> whatever email address you use, and are not asked for a GMC number. Review usually takes 1–2
> working days.

## Logging In

Go to the login page and enter your email and password. If you have forgotten your password, click **Forgot password?** to receive a reset link by email.

## Next Steps

Once approved, head to the [Member Portal](member-portal.md) to set up your profile and explore features. See [Membership Tiers](membership-tiers.md) to understand what each tier unlocks.
